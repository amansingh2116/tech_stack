\textbf{How to use this document.} This guide synthesizes a large, growing set of source material into one
system, covered in order: \textbf{(1)} the mindset and market reality you need first, including the cultural and
behavioral gaps that block otherwise-qualified candidates; \textbf{(2)} the mathematical model behind why
outreach works at all; \textbf{(3)} two parallel proof-of-work tracks --- production-grade projects and
open-source contributions; \textbf{(4)} how to package that proof through LinkedIn and positioning; \textbf{(5)}
a complete cold-outreach toolkit --- universal principles, templates by audience (founders, recruiters,
professors, internships, freelance clients), the sourcing tools and AI search agents to execute it, and a full
operational case study at scale; and \textbf{(6)} what happens after you land the role --- getting paid legally
as a remote contractor from India. A 16-week roadmap (with a parallel student-focused path), consolidated
checklists, and a full template library close out the document. Boxes are used consistently throughout:
\textcolor{goodline}{\textbf{green}} for takeaways, \textcolor{warnline}{\textbf{red}} for reality checks and
caveats, and \textcolor{tmplline}{\textbf{blue}} for copy-paste templates.
