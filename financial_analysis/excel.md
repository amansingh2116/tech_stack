# Excel

## 8. Introduction to MS Excel

Microsoft Excel is a premier tool for data and business analytics. It allows you to organize, calculate, and analyze numerical data for tasks ranging from personal budgeting to high-level financial decision-making.

### Key Terminology

* **Spreadsheet:** The grid-like interface where you input and manipulate data.

* **Workbook:** A collection of one or more spreadsheets.

* **Ribbon Tabs:** The menu bar at the top containing various functional categories (Home, Insert, etc.).

* **Cell Address:** The unique identifier for a cell, combining its **Column Name** (letters) and **Row Number** (numbers) (e.g., **C18**).

---

## 9. File Management & The "File" Tab

The **File** tab (also known as the Backstage view) is used for administrative tasks.

* **New:** Create a **Blank Workbook** or download pre-made **Templates** like formula tutorials, budget trackers, or calendars.

* **Open:** Access existing documents from your PC or via **Browse**.

* **Save vs. Save As:**

  * **Save:** Updates the current file.

  * **Save As:** Saves a copy with a new name or in a different format, such as a **PDF**.

* **Print & Export:** Use these to physicalize your data or send it to other platforms.

---

## 10. Core Functions: The Home Tab

The Home tab contains the most frequently used tools for data entry and formatting.

### Clipboard & Formatting

* **Cut ($Ctrl + X$):** Moves the original text or object to a new location.

* **Copy ($Ctrl + C$):** Duplicates the original data without modifying its original position.

* **Paste ($Ctrl + V$):** Places the cut or copied item into the selected cell.

* **Format Painter:** Allows you to copy the visual formatting (color, font, etc.) of one cell and apply it to another.

### Font & Alignment

* **Font Styles:** Change the typeface (e.g., Calibri) or adjust the **Font Size** using the dropdown or the "Increase/Decrease" buttons.

* **Emphasis:** Use **Bold**, *Italics*, or Underline to highlight specific data.

* **Colors:** Use **Font Color** to change text hue and **Fill Color** to highlight the entire cell background.

* **Alignment:**

  * **Horizontal:** Left, Center, or Right.

  * **Vertical:** Top, Middle, or Bottom (useful if row height is increased).

  * **Orientation:** Rotate text diagonally or vertically.

  * **Indentation:** Increase or decrease the distance between the cell border and the text.

### Text & Cell Manipulation

* **Wrap Text:** Forces long text to stay within one cell by increasing the row height instead of overflowing into adjacent cells.

* **Merge & Center:** Combines multiple selected cells into one large cell and centers the text—ideal for creating table titles.

## 11. Data Types & Number Formatting

To ensure Excel calculates data correctly, you must define the **Number Format** in the Home tab.

* **General:** The default format for mixed data.

* **Number:** Used for standard numerical values.

* **Currency/Accounting:** Adds symbols (like $) and proper decimal alignment for financial amounts.

* **Comma Style:** Automatically adds thousands-separators (e.g., turning 10000 into 10,000).

* **Decimal Control:** Use the **Increase/Decrease Decimal** buttons to manage the precision of your numbers.

---

## 12. Working with the Insert Tab

The **Insert** tab is used to add external elements to your spreadsheet.

* **Illustrations:** Add **Shapes**, **Pictures**, **3D Models**, or **Screenshots**.

* **Text Box:** Creates a floating box for typing notes that aren't confined to cells.

* **Charts:** Transform data into visual formats like **2D Bar Charts**.

* **X-Axis:** Typically represents categories (e.g., Food, Rent).

* **Y-Axis:** Represents the numerical values or amounts.

---

## 13. Advanced Viewing & Layout

The **View** tab helps you manage how you see your work.

* **Display Options:** You can toggle the **Headings** (row/column labels), **Gridlines**, and the **Formula Bar** on or off.

* **Zoom:** Adjust the scale of the sheet from 100% to a custom level.

* **Freezing Panes:**

  1. Select the cells you wish to remain visible while scrolling.

  2. Click **Freeze Panes**.

  3. This keeps headers (like company names or logos) at the top or specific columns on the left as you navigate large datasets.

  4. Click **Unfreeze Panes** to return to normal scrolling.
